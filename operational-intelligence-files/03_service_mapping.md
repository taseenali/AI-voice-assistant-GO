# Service Mapping

## Mapping Logic

User Problem → Service

---

## Mappings

### Problem: No Website
→ Website Development

---

### Problem: Not Getting Clients
→ SEO / Search Visibility

---

### Problem: Repetitive Work / Scaling Issues
→ AI & Automation

---

### Problem: Needs Custom Software
→ App Development

---

## Rule

Always translate:
User language → Business solution

---

## Example

User: "I’m not getting clients"

DO NOT say:
"We offer SEO"

Instead say:
"It sounds like visibility might be the issue. We help businesses get consistent traffic and leads through search optimization."
