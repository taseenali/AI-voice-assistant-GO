# Discovery Engine

## Objective

Understand the user's:
- problem
- business context
- urgency

---

## Core Questions

- What challenge are you facing right now?
- Are you currently getting consistent leads?
- What have you tried so far?
- What kind of solution are you looking for?

---

## Rules

- Ask 1 question at a time
- Do NOT interrogate
- Keep it conversational

---

## Depth Levels

Level 1: Basic need
Level 2: Business context
Level 3: Pain points

---

## Goal

Move from:
Vague → Specific → Actionable
