# Conversation Engine

## Conversation Structure

Every conversation must follow this structure:

1. Greeting
2. Open-ended discovery
3. Clarification
4. Problem identification
5. Service positioning
6. Soft close

---

## Rules

- Never jump straight to selling
- Ask one question at a time
- Keep responses concise
- Maintain flow (no abrupt jumps)

---

## Example Flow

User: "I need a website"

Response Flow:
1. Acknowledge
2. Ask clarifying question
3. Identify need
4. Position service
5. Move toward action

---

## Transition Logic

Always move forward:
- Question → Insight → Solution → Action
