\section{Market Context}

The medical AI receptionist market in 2026 is consolidating around three architectures:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Reasoning AI platforms} (Hyro, Claire) --- enterprise, deep EHR
  \item \textbf{Virtual receptionist + AI} (Smith.ai, Ruby) --- hybrid human backup
  \item \textbf{Developer platforms} (Vapi, Retell) --- build-your-own on API
\end{enumerate}

MedVoice occupies category 1+3: \textbf{medical-specialized product built on developer-grade runtime}---faster to ship than pure platform, more control than boxed SMB tools.

\section{Why Clinics Buy Beyond Price}

These are your \textbf{privileges}---reasons to choose MedVoice at premium vs.\ \$65/mo generic tools:

\subsection{1. Medical-Specific Safety (non-negotiable)}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item Deterministic emergency keyword gate (bypasses LLM) --- \verified in codebase
  \item Red-flag routing to clinical staff within seconds
  \item HIPAA BAA chain managed for clinic (one vendor relationship)
  \item Audit trail for every call turn (compliance-ready)
\end{itemize}

\subsection{2. Appointment Intelligence}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item Real-time Google Calendar freebusy (not just Calendly links)
  \item Structured lead capture: DOB, insurance, reason for visit
  \item Service-specific discovery flows (dental, urgent, follow-up)
  \item Future: no-show recovery outbound campaigns
\end{itemize}

\subsection{3. After-Hours Revenue Recovery}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item 62\% of patient calls occur outside business hours (industry avg)
  \item Voicemail = lost appointment; MedVoice books at 2am
  \item Quantify ROI: ``3 after-hours bookings/mo $\times$ \$200 visit = \$600 recovered''
\end{itemize}

\subsection{4. Zero Staff Turnover Tax}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item Human receptionist: \$5K recruiting + 3-month ramp per hire
  \item MedVoice: consistent greeting, zero sick days, instant scale
\end{itemize}

\subsection{5. Multilingual Without Interpreter Line}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item Spanish (Phase 3) included --- no \$0.50/min interpreter fees
  \item Same medical terminology accuracy across languages
\end{itemize}

\subsection{6. Clinic-Owned Data}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item BYOK provider keys --- clinic keeps vendor relationships
  \item Webhook to clinic's n8n/CRM --- data never trapped in platform
  \item Export all transcripts, leads, recordings on demand
\end{itemize}

\subsection{7. Transparent Dashboard}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item Real-time lead feed, emergency alerts, call transcripts
  \item No black-box AI --- see exactly what was said and captured
  \item Per-service conversion metrics (Phase 4)
\end{itemize}

\section{Sales Narrative (Elevator to Close)}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Problem:} ``Your front desk misses 30--40\% of after-hours calls. Each missed call is a \$150--300 lost visit.''
  \item \textbf{Solution:} ``MedVoice answers every call in 2 rings, books into your real calendar, and escalates emergencies to your on-call line.''
  \item \textbf{Proof:} Live demo call to their Google Calendar; show dashboard transcript.
  \item \textbf{Safety:} ``Our emergency detector doesn't use AI---it's a hard-coded medical red-flag system.''
  \item \textbf{ROI:} ``At \$2,500/mo you need 13 extra appointments to break even. We typically see 20--40.''
  \item \textbf{Close:} Founding Practice pilot at \$999/mo, 30-day money-back if $<$5 bookings.
\end{enumerate}
